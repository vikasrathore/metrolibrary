About

A quiet space built for focus

Metro Library is a dedicated reading room in Alipur Khera, Mainpuri, built for students and competitive exam aspirants who need a calm, comfortable place to study. With full air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, and quiet seating, we give you the environment to sit down, focus, and get real study hours in.
