About

Serving readers and lifelong learners

Metro Library is a community library offering a growing collection of books, digital resources, and a welcoming space to study and connect. Whether you're here to borrow a novel, do research, or attend a workshop, our doors are open.

Services

What we offer

Book Lending

A curated collection spanning fiction, non-fiction, and reference titles for every age group.

Study Spaces

Quiet reading rooms and shared tables designed for focus and collaboration.

Digital Access

Free Wi-Fi and public computers for research, e-books, and online learning.

Community Events

Reading circles, workshops, and programs for kids, students, and adults alike.
